# Surface brief: Plein Sud, écran principal (web/index.html)

Mode: Operate. Audience: owner (CFMoto 450MT) and friends (moto, scooter, car) around Montpellier. Job: at a red light, read in two seconds where to go, the price per litre, the distance and the full-tank cost, then tap one big button to navigate. Calmly at home: compare, map, favourites, savings log. Code-led. Name "Plein Sud"; logo direction pending (provisional: compass-flower).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A fuel finder that looks like nothing from the fuel world: an embroidered floral tee, cream cloth by day, night flowers by night. One answer, stitched large; refuses the category's grey list of price pins and the previous roadbook.

OWN-WORLD: Light: cream cloth ground, warm dark-thread ink, threads of dusty rose, lavender blue, coral, ochre, sage and pale sky. Dark "fleurs de nuit": deep night-blue cloth, cream thread text, silver, gold and softened colour threads. Young Serif for station names and headings, Archivo condensed for figures. Art is hand-authored SVG line embroidery (running stitch dashes, satin hatching, French knots), no glyph icons.

STORY: The rider sees the best station, its price, distance and full-tank cost, taps "Y aller" and lands in Plans, Google Maps or Waze. Two alternatives follow, the full list on demand. Staying 3 minutes at a station brings up a note-your-fill card from the Carnet tab.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim top bar (mark, name, freshness, settings). Fuel segmented control. Answer card: name, giant price, distance and full-tank cost side by side, full-width "Y aller" button; the bouquet climbs the card's right edge. Below: two compact alternatives and "Voir toutes les stations". Standard bottom tabs: Stations, Carte, Favoris, Carnet.

FORM: Brief-pinned by the user (Derschutze-style embroidered florals on cream; "fleurs de nuit" for dark), replacing the roadbook direction; no new roll. seed: none (unavailable). Signature move: the bouquet stitches itself upward on first load (clip reveal) and the answer card re-stitches when the best station changes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
